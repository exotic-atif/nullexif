Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Created by Atif Arman (Exotic Atif)

The Short Version: NullExif is built with respect for your privacy. We do not sell your data, we do not track you across the web, and we do not store your photos permanently on our servers. What you do with your photos is strictly your business.

1. Who We Are

NullExif is an open utility built and maintained by Atif Arman (also known as Exotic Atif), an independent digital creator and developer. Official portfolio: exoticatif.likesyou.org. Source code: github.com/exotic-atif/nullexif.

2. What Data We Process & Why

  • Uploaded Photos: When you drag or upload photos to inspect, edit, or remove EXIF data, they are processed solely to perform the actions you explicitly trigger. We do not use your photos to train AI models, do not catalog them, and do not share them with any third parties.
  • Temporary Storage: Uploaded files exist in temporary workspace directories only long enough to process and download your results. You can click the "Clear" button in the app at any time to purge uploaded images immediately.

3. Google OAuth & Google Photos API (Google User Data)

If you choose to use the optional "Add to Google Photos" feature, NullExif connects to Google APIs via official OAuth 2.0. Here is exactly how Google user data is handled:

  • Requested Scopes:
    • photoslibrary.appendonly: Allows NullExif to upload only the photo you choose directly to your own Google Photos library. We cannot view, delete, or modify your existing photos or albums.
    • userinfo.email: Allows the app to display which Google account you are currently connected to in the interface.
  • No Plaintext Tokens on Disk: Your OAuth access token and refresh token are stored exclusively in your isolated browser session (via an encrypted, HTTP-only session cookie). They are never saved to public database tables or plain text server files.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: NullExif will NEVER sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies.
  • Revoking Access & Sign Out: You can click "Sign out" in the app header at any time, which immediately destroys your session and clears all tokens. You can also revoke NullExif's access permanently from your Google Account Permissions page.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

NullExif does not use marketing cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytical cookies. We only use:

  • A standard session cookie (PHPSESSID) marked Secure and HttpOnly to keep you logged in to Google Photos during your visit.
  • Your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save your interface theme preference (Light / Dark mode).

5. Security of Your Data

All communications between your browser and NullExif are protected by industry-standard HTTPS encryption. We enforce strict transport security and secure headers to keep your data safe in transit.
