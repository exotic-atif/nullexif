Engineered for Creators & Photographers

Everything you need to audit, polish, or sanitize image metadata in one intuitive workspace.

Deep EXIF Inspection & Editing

Read and update camera make, model, lens profile, ISO, aperture (f-number), shutter speed, focal length, software, and original capture timestamps.

GPS Geolocation Mapping

Easily correct or assign GPS coordinates. Supports decimal degrees (e.g. 28.6139, 77.2090) and degrees-minutes-seconds (DMS) with Google Maps integration.

1-Click EXIF Stripping

Remove sensitive location tags, camera serial numbers, and device footprints before publishing photos online to protect personal privacy.

Format Conversion with EXIF Sync

Convert seamlessly between JPEG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC without losing vital metadata headers or image quality.

Direct Google Photos Upload

Upload processed photos directly to your personal Google Photos library using an isolated, secure OAuth 2.0 session.

Zero Data Retention

Your photos are processed transiently and never stored permanently on disk. No tracking scripts, no advertising, and no database logging.

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers about NullExif and how it works.

Is NullExif completely free to use?

Yes, NullExif is 100% free and open-source under the stewardship of Exotic Atif. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or hidden charges.

Does NullExif store my uploaded photos?

No. Photos are processed purely on demand in temporary server buffers for metadata extraction and conversion. You can clear them at any time with the "Clear" button.

How does the Google Photos connection work?

We use official Google OAuth 2.0 with the restricted photoslibrary.appendonly scope. NullExif can only upload photos you explicitly choose; it cannot view, delete, or modify your existing photo albums.

Which image formats are supported?

NullExif handles standard JPEG/JPG, PNG, modern WebP, and Apple HEIC/HEIF photos with native EXIF metadata retention.
